# NNNN. Short title of the decision

- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Decided by:** name(s)
- **Task / issue:** e.g. D-11 (#42)
- **Status:** decided | replaced by NNNN

## Context
What problem or question forced a choice. One paragraph, with the numbers or facts that mattered.

## Options
1. Option A: what it is, its main advantage and cost.
2. Option B: …

## Decision
What we chose, in one sentence.

## Why
The reason, tied to evidence (a result file, a measurement, a course concept with its source in docs/COURSE-GROUNDING.md, a syllabus rule).

## Consequences
What changes because of it, what we give up, what to watch.
